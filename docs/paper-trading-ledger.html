How to read this.
  • v3's small losses came mostly from sitting in cash. v4 seats are 60–90% invested, so in a month when their names fell they lost more. This is the first version where the returns reflect what the seats picked.
  • RISK's 5% trailing stop fired 21 times on a 20-name defensive book. Open question D5: try 8% before changing the policy.
  • Without the 7-day stop cooldown, RISK re-bought 15 names within five days of their stop. The cooldown now blocks that.
  • Limits: one decision a day, not four. No model arbitration calls. Fills at the daily close. One month is one sample.
