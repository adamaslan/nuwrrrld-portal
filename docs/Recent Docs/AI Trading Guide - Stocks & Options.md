# Using AI to Buy & Sell Stocks and Options
### A practical guide + the 10 best AI-powered apps (2026)

> ⚠️ **Not financial advice.** AI tools improve your process — they don't guarantee profits. Options can lose 100% of premium fast. Never trade money you can't afford to lose, and always paper trade a new AI workflow before going live.

---

## Part 1: How AI Actually Helps You Trade

AI won't hand you winning trades. What it does well is compress hours of grunt work into minutes and remove emotion from decisions. Here's where it fits in a real trading workflow:

### 1. Idea Generation & Screening
Instead of manually scanning thousands of tickers, AI screeners rank stocks by probability of outperforming — using hundreds of technical, fundamental, and sentiment features. You start each day with a shortlist instead of a blank chart.

### 2. Technical Analysis
AI charting tools auto-detect trendlines, support/resistance, chart patterns, and multi-timeframe confluences that humans miss or draw inconsistently. It's the same analysis you'd do by hand — just faster and without bias.

### 3. Signal Generation
Real-time AI engines watch price action, volume, and news across the whole market and fire alerts when setups matching historical high-probability patterns appear. You define the risk; the AI does the watching.

### 4. Options Strategy Selection
For options, AI helps you answer: *which strategy fits my outlook?* Tools model probability of profit, expected move, Greeks exposure, and payoff curves across strategies (spreads, condors, covered calls) so you compare setups objectively instead of guessing.

### 5. Automation & Backtesting
No-code bot builders let you encode rules ("sell a 30-delta put spread when IV rank > 50, close at 50% profit") and backtest them against years of data before risking a dollar. The AI executes without hesitation, FOMO, or revenge trading.

### 6. Sentiment & News Analysis
LLM-powered assistants digest earnings calls, filings, news, and social chatter in seconds and summarize what actually matters for your positions.

### 7. Review & Journaling
AI trade journals analyze your fill history and tell you uncomfortable truths: which setups you're actually profitable on, when you overtrade, and where you bleed money.

## Part 3: A Sample AI-Powered Workflow

**For stocks (swing trading):**
1. **Morning:** Check Danelfin's top-scored stocks → shortlist 3–5 names.
2. **Analysis:** Pull them up in TrendSpider → let AI mark levels and patterns → keep only clean setups.
3. **Confirmation:** Ask Public's Alpha or moomoo's AI "why is this stock moving, and what are the risks this week?"
4. **Execution:** Enter via your broker with a predefined stop. Set TrendSpider alerts instead of screen-watching.
5. **Review:** Log the trade; weekly, ask an AI journal what your win rate is by setup type.

**For options (income strategy):**
1. **Screen:** Use OptionStrat to find high-IV candidates and model a credit spread — check probability of profit and max loss.
2. **Validate:** Cross-check the underlying's AI score (Danelfin) and trend (TrendSpider).
3. **Automate:** Encode the rules in Option Alpha — entry at IV rank > 50, exit at 50% profit or 21 DTE, stop at 2x credit.
4. **Backtest first.** Only deploy live after the backtest and 2–4 weeks of paper trading hold up.

## Part 4: Rules for Trading with AI

1. **AI is a copilot, not an autopilot.** Every tool above has losing streaks. You own the risk decisions.
2. **Backtest, then paper trade, then size small.** In that order, always.
3. **Beware backtest overfitting.** A strategy tuned perfectly to the past often fails in the future. Prefer simple rules that work across market regimes.
4. **Position sizing beats prediction.** No AI signal justifies risking more than 1–2% of your account on one trade.
5. **Options decay is real.** AI can pick direction well and you can still lose to theta and IV crush. Always model the full payoff (OptionStrat) before entering.
6. **Verify AI claims.** "90% win rate" bots often have tiny wins and catastrophic losses. Look at profit factor and max drawdown, not win rate.
7. **Costs compound.** $200/mo in subscriptions needs to earn $2,400/yr just to break even. Start with free tiers (OptionStrat, Danelfin, Option Alpha, Public, moomoo) and upgrade only what proves its value.
