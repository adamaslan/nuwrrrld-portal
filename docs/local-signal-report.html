How to read this. Score is indicator agreement 0–100 from confluence(rsi, macdCross, adx): RSI ≤ 30 or a MACD bullish cross votes up, RSI ≥ 70 or a MACD bearish cross votes down, and an ADX ≥ 25 trending tape amplifies the agreement by 1.25×. Direction is the sign of the net vote. A wall of zeros over a short window is expected — the indicators need history before they say anything, and a daily MACD cross is a rare event.
